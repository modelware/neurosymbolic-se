# NSEE Capstone Roadmap

Modelware · Neurosymbolic Systems Engineering (NSEE)

Build one engineering model through four cumulative milestones. Module numbers describe progression through the material; they do not imply a weekly timetable.

| Milestone | Modules | Outcome | Ready for review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [1: Scope and questions](Module1.md#capstone-milestone-1-module-1-project-scope--problem-statement) | 1 | System boundary, stakeholders, 3–5 engineering questions, difficulty, and source data | Before beginning Module 2 |
| [2: Representation and reasoning](Module2.md#capstone-milestone-2-modules-2-3-module-2-component-initial-vocabulary-and-description) | 2–3 | Initial vocabulary and descriptions, bundles, reasoning features, and explained results | After Module 3 |
| [3: Methodology and analysis](Module4.md#capstone-milestone-3-modules-4-5-module-4-component-turn-your-vocabulary-into-a-small-methodology) | 4–5 | Authoring patterns, validation, editors, reusable templates, queries, dashboards, and diagnosed gaps | After Module 5 |
| [4: Grounded AI and a controlled release](Module6.md#capstone-milestone-4-modules-6-8-module-6-component-put-an-agent-to-work-on-your-method) | 6–8 | Agent comparison, improved guidance, a generator, a reusable package, an independent consumer, and verified release `1.0.0` | After Module 8 |

For a milestone spanning several modules, preserve the evidence from each component and review the cumulative result at its end. Keep the exact repository revisions that support your findings. Module 7 introduces a separate consumer repository; record its version relationship to the methodology repository.

Your final report should trace each Module 1 question to evidence in release `1.0.0`, or explain the diagnosed gap and what would be needed to resolve it. Follow the review and delivery arrangements in the [course guide](CourseGuide.md).
